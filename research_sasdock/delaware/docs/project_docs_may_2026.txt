Alternative models
Concanavalin A (25.5 kDa/monomer – dimer at low pH, tetramer at high pH). Available at ThermoFisher, Sigma (eg. L7647); needs CaCl2 and MnCl2 for stability (prevent aggregation). Needs decent amount of salt (100 mM or higher) to stay soluble.
Aldolase (~160 kDa; tetramer); from my understanding, if we chose a class I aldolase, it does not require co-factors for stability. Class II requires metal cofactors, but may be more stable. Looks like there are suppliers at a reasonable price; https://moleculardepot.com/product/aldolase-lyophilized-2/
Sorbitol Dehydrogenase (152 kDa; tetramer). Similar to LDH in structure and co-factors. Also a model protein. Looks more reasonable in terms of price/suppliers but we’d need to check if it’s stable, if it needs zinc etc. https://moleculardepot.com/product/sorbitol-dehydrogenase-sdh/
Glucose isomerase (173 kDa; tetramer). Looks expensive, similar to LDH in prices
Urate oxidase (4x 33 kDa tetramer). Very interesting in terms of thermal stability but looks expensive, similar to LDH in prices

AlphaFold2-Multimer
and AlphaFold3 (AF3) effectively predict protein self-association (homooligomerization) and quaternary structures, with the interface predicted TM-score (ipTM) acting as a reliable indicator of correct assembly. Both tools can distinguish between monomeric and multimeric proteins, with AF3 showing higher accuracy in some cases, although 3-5 recycles are often necessary to confirm stoichiometry
Key Details on AlphaFold and LDH
Structure Availability: The AlphaFold Database contains entries for many species (e.g., B. longum, S. aureus, X. laevis).
Model Quality: LDH structures generally show high confidence (often >90% pLDDT), making them reliable for downstream structural analysis.
Self-Association: While standard AlphaFold predicts monomers, using AlphaFold-Multimer (often via ColabFold) allows for the prediction of the quaternary structure (e.g., the tetramer) of LDH, note.
Functional Context: LDHs are key enzymes in metabolizing lactate to pyruvate.
Application: Researchers can use these models to study how mutations or small molecules, such as fructose 1,6-bisphosphate, affect enzyme assembly.
